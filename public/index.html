04 · The game

Race your group.
Battle your friends.

RACE

Weekly groups

Join up to 15 players at about your activity level and race for the week, by steps or running distance. When it ends, everyone gets a roll to collect a copy of another player's card. Top three can turn their card foil.

COLLECT

Build a deck

Every card you collect goes into your deck. The higher you finish, the better your odds of landing a great one.

BATTLE

Cards head to head

Challenge a friend and your cards go stat by stat. Win and you take a copy of theirs. A happy, active creature fights harder than a tired one.

EARN

Coins & backgrounds

Earn coins just by being active and spend them on card backgrounds, including animated ones. Some drop for a limited time.

EVENTS

Seasonal showdowns

Limited-time events split the community into sides, hand out limited badges, and reward the winning team with a background nobody else can get.

BADGES

Every milestone

Your first 10,000 steps, a marathon distance, a 100-day streak. Some badges even give your creature a permanent boost.

05 · Built for everyone

Play it your way.
Stay in control.

For players 13 and up. Younger players get extra protection: a random public name, no photos and private profiles by default. Everyone can keep their profile private, turn off friend requests, block other players, and delete their account and data from Settings at any time.
